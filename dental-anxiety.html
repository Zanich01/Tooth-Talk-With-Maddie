A more comfortable dental visit

You can ask for support before you sit in the chair.

Start before the appointment

Tell the office you feel anxious when you book. Explain what worries you, whether that is discomfort, sounds, uncertainty, or a past experience.

Make a plan together

Ask your dental team to explain the steps and agree on a signal, such as raising your hand, when you need a pause. Ask about breaks and comfort options.

Give yourself something to focus on

Slow breathing or listening to music may help you relax. Ask whether headphones are appropriate during your appointment. Your team can discuss other options based on your needs and health history.

A simple way to start

“I am nervous about this visit. Can we talk about what will happen and how I can ask for a break?”
