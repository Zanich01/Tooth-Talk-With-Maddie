About this information

Educational disclaimer

Use Tooth Talk with Maddie to learn about oral health and prepare questions for your dental team. Your own care requires an individual assessment.

Updated October 4, 2026

General education, not individual care

All content on this website, including articles, illustrations, answers to common questions, search results, and product guides, is for general informational and educational purposes. It is not a substitute for professional dental or medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment.

Consult your dentist or another qualified healthcare professional about your symptoms, medical history, and individual needs. Do not disregard professional advice or delay seeking care because of something you read on this website. Do not use this website to diagnose yourself or choose treatment for a condition.

No provider–patient relationship

Reading or using this website does not establish a provider–patient relationship with Madison Migliore, RDH, or any other healthcare professional. Descriptions of dental services are general; permitted services and supervision requirements vary by location and setting. This website does not provide examinations, individualized treatment plans, or emergency care.

If you need urgent care

If you believe you are experiencing a medical emergency, call 911 in the United States or your local emergency number. For a dental emergency, contact your dentist or seek urgent care promptly. Do not wait for information or a response from this website.

Read the American Dental Association’s dental emergency guidance.

Product information and outside links

Product listings are general examples of oral care options, not prescriptions or a determination that a product is safe or suitable for you. Individual needs and results vary. Follow the current manufacturer’s label, directions, age restrictions, and warnings. Ask your dental team about suitability, especially if you have a health condition or are considering whitening or products for ongoing symptoms.

Product formulations, availability, prices, and retailer information can change. Check those details before buying. Links to outside websites are provided for convenience; Tooth Talk with Maddie does not control their content, policies, products, or services. A link does not imply a professional organization endorses this website or its product selections.

Sources, updates, and outcomes

We aim to provide accurate, useful information, but content may contain errors or become outdated as guidance changes. Source-check dates indicate when references were checked; they do not mean a clinician has reviewed or approved the page unless that review is explicitly stated. No particular health outcome or product result is guaranteed.

Examples and illustrations simplify information for learning and may not reflect your circumstances. Your dental team’s assessment and individualized recommendations should guide your care.
