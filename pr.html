Daily cleaning+

How to chooseElectric brushes offer another way to brush; choose one that feels easy to use.

Keep it simpleA soft manual toothbrush and fluoride toothpaste are a simple place to start.

Additional gum care+

How to chooseA water flosser can help with more thorough cleaning beneath the gums. Maddie recommends using a water flosser as additional gum care, alongside daily string flossing—not as a replacement for it. Ask your hygienist which device and technique suit you.

Keep it simpleOrdinary floss is a simple option; ask about interdental brushes if those fit your spaces.

Dry mouth comfort+

How to chooseRinses, lozenges, and adhering tablets offer different formats. Follow their labels.

Keep it simpleKeep water handy. Ask your dental team about ongoing dryness before buying multiple products.

Fresh breath+

How to chooseA tongue cleaner or rinse can be an extra step, depending on your needs.

Keep it simplePrioritize brushing and cleaning between teeth; persistent bad breath deserves an assessment.

Whitening+

How to chooseStrips, prefilled trays, and gel have different directions and wear times.

Keep it simpleWhitening is optional. Ask about suitability before spending; avoid layering whitening products.

Build your routine around brushing and cleaning between teeth. Mouthrinse can be an addition, but does not replace those steps or professional treatment for gum disease. Ask your dental team which rinse fits your needs.

Bad Breath

Persistent bad breath is a reason to check in with your dental team. Tongue cleaning and an appropriate rinse may be part of your routine; a rinse alone may only mask an underlying problem. Bad breath can be related to poor oral hygiene, gum disease, or tonsil stones. A clinician can help determine the cause.

Whitening

Whitening schedules depend on the product and concentration. Follow its directions and discuss suitability with your dentist. Sensitivity and gum irritation can occur. Whitening toothpastes mainly remove surface stains; bleaching changes natural tooth color but does not whiten restorations.
