Understand your smile

More than a cleaning

Meet the prevention, assessment, and education behind a dental hygienist’s work.

The key takeaway

Your hygienist helps you understand your oral health and make a plan for everyday care.

What you can do today

Review your medication list before your next visit.

01

Assess and understand

Hygienists assess oral health, look for signs of disease, take dental X-rays, and share findings with the dentist. They document care so the team can follow changes over time.

02

Clean and protect

Removing plaque, tartar, and stains is part of preventive care. Hygienists may also apply fluoride or sealants to help protect teeth. Permitted services and supervision requirements vary by state and setting.

03

Make home care practical

Your hygienist focuses on prevention, assessment, and home-care education. The dentist diagnoses conditions and plans restorative treatment; their roles work together. Permitted responsibilities vary by state.

04

Make the most of your visit

A visit may include a health-history update, an examination, a gum assessment, and cleaning. Your dentist recommends X-rays based on your oral health, examination, and individual cavity risk. The recommended frequency may change as your risk changes. Share symptoms, medications, and anything that makes home care difficult. Ask when you should return based on your needs.
