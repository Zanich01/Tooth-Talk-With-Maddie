Understanding dry mouth

When your mouth feels persistently dry, look beyond a quick rinse.

Why saliva matters

Saliva helps protect teeth and makes chewing and swallowing easier. Too little saliva can increase the risk of decay and mouth infections.

Look for the cause

Medicines and some health conditions can reduce saliva. Tell your dental and medical teams about persistent dryness. Discuss medication concerns with the prescriber before making changes.

Comfort and prevention work together

Sips of water may help with comfort. Ask your dental team about saliva substitutes and a prevention plan suited to your risk. A product that makes your mouth feel better does not replace finding the cause.
