Understand your smile

A healthier smile. A healthier you.

Oral health is part of whole-person care. Here is how to understand the connections without the alarm.

The key takeaway

A link between two conditions does not prove that one causes the other.

01

What the research tells us

Researchers study links between gum disease and conditions including cardiovascular disease and pregnancy complications. Shared risk factors and inflammation may help explain these associations. Evidence of an association does not establish that dental treatment prevents those other conditions.

02

Diabetes and your mouth

Diabetes raises the likelihood of gum disease and may slow healing. Gum disease can also make blood sugar harder to manage. Share your diabetes history with your dental team and continue the care plan from your medical team.

03

Keep your care team connected

Bring an up-to-date medication list and tell your dental team about health changes, pregnancy, and ongoing treatment. Ask how these may affect your oral care.

04

Focus on what you can do

Use your appointments to discuss symptoms and build a routine you can maintain. Dental care supports your oral health; it does not replace medical treatment.
