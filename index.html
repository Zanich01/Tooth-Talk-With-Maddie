Good habits, made practical

Your daily care, step by step.

Three simple walkthroughs for your everyday routine. Open a card to see the steps.

01 / Everyday care

Brushing basics

Two minutes. Twice a day.

See the steps +
  1. Choose a soft brush and fluoride toothpaste.
  2. With a manual brush, angle the bristles toward the gumline at about 45 degrees and use gentle, short strokes.
  3. Cover outside, inside, and chewing surfaces. Follow the instructions for your electric brush.
Read the ADA guide ↗
02 / Everyday care

Flossing basics

A gentle curve around each tooth.

See the steps +
  1. Guide the floss between teeth without snapping it into the gums.
  2. Curve it around one tooth in a C shape, then move gently up and down along the side.
  3. Clean the neighboring tooth too, and repeat around your mouth, including the backs of the last teeth.
Read the ADA guide ↗
03 / Everyday care

Water flossing

Another way to clean between teeth.

See the steps +
  1. A water flosser may help when floss is difficult to handle or around certain dental work.
  2. Follow the setup, tip, and pressure instructions for your particular device.
  3. Ask your hygienist to demonstrate the technique for your mouth. Clean between teeth daily.

A few things you might be wondering

Common questions

Simple answers to everyday oral care questions.

Explore the full guides →
Do I need an electric toothbrush?

Start with a soft brush you can use comfortably and consistently. Your dental team can help you choose between a manual and electric brush. Learn more

Should I floss before or after brushing?

Either order works. The key is cleaning thoroughly between teeth every day. Pick a time you can stick with. Learn more

Can mouthwash replace brushing or flossing?

No. A rinse may complement your routine, but brushing and cleaning between teeth remain the foundations. Learn more

What if my gums bleed?

Bleeding can be a sign of gum inflammation. Arrange a dental assessment to understand the cause and ask for help with your technique. Learn more
